# Horyzon website narrative redesign

## Purpose

Redesign the Italian public website so it explains Horyzon as an integrated business-transformation system rather than as three adjacent consulting areas. The website must connect the founder's strategic horizon to diagnosis, organizational design, implementation, digital enablement and measurable governance.

The public story must be grounded in the products that exist today:

- Horyzon Hub conducts discovery, manages evidence and operational study, and produces a versioned Blueprint.
- Radar d'Impresa is a public Hub flow that qualifies the company and assesses five operating areas, process maturity, owner autonomy and AI adoption.
- Horyzon Platform is a tenant-isolated customer dashboard for KPIs, reports, snapshots, synchronization status and activation evidence.
- ChatGPT Work and approved external systems perform operational work; Platform does not claim to operate customer systems directly.

## Product truth and communication boundaries

### Available now

- Public Radar at `https://hub.horyzon.it/radar`.
- Qualification form followed by the assessment flow.
- Five-area profile: administration, production, sales, marketing and people.
- Scores for organizational maturity, owner autonomy, overall maturity and AI adoption.
- Sector-aware interpretation, strongest area, priority area and a request for a Horyzon debrief.
- Hub discovery and Blueprint workflow.
- Platform dashboard, reporting, activation status and tenant-scoped governance foundations.

### Configured during a Horyzon engagement

- Target organization chart and comparison with the current organization.
- Priority department and transformation programme.
- Roles, procedures, metrics, tools and operating responsibilities.
- Blueprint and activation plan, reviewed before activation.
- Dashboard configuration and data synchronization appropriate to the company.

### Vision, not guaranteed outcome

- A company progressively less dependent on the founder.
- Departments capable of operating through shared responsibilities and repeatable processes.
- AI used as a governed work layer, not as an autonomous decision maker.
- Recognition of people's contribution through visible outcomes.

The website must not guarantee owner independence within a fixed time, automatic profitability, complete automation, or capabilities that have not been activated and tested. Gamification is described as visibility and recognition of contribution, not employee surveillance or competition for raw task volume.

## Recommended narrative architecture

The site should follow one continuous logic:

`Entrepreneurial horizon -> Radar -> current-state diagnosis -> target organization -> priority department -> operating programme -> people and AI -> Platform governance -> measured evolution`

The current three dimensions remain useful, but their hierarchy changes:

- Organizational wellbeing is the core transformation discipline.
- Digital wellbeing is the enabling and governance layer across the entire model.
- Financial wellbeing protects continuity and the value created by the business.

## Information architecture

### Primary navigation

1. How Horyzon works
2. Radar d'Impresa
3. The business system
4. Platform
5. People
6. Insights
7. Contact

The visual identity, existing scroll-linked video and editorial design remain. Navigation labels become outcome-oriented and easier for an entrepreneur to understand.

### Homepage

The homepage remains cinematic, but every chapter answers a concrete question.

1. **Horizon** — the entrepreneur defines where the company must go.
2. **Current state** — growth often leaves the owner at the centre of every decision.
3. **Radar** — a real assessment of five departments, process maturity, owner autonomy and AI adoption.
4. **Business organism** — departments operate as interdependent organs.
5. **Transformation path** — target organization, priority department, implementation and verification.
6. **Operating infrastructure** — Hub, people, ChatGPT Work and Platform each have a distinct role.
7. **Visible progress** — KPIs, reports, activities, synchronization and activation evidence.
8. **Next action** — start the Radar or request a conversation.

The hero must include a visible direct action to the Radar. A second Radar invitation appears when the diagnostic chapter becomes visible. External-link treatment makes clear that the assessment opens in the Horyzon Hub environment.

### Radar d'Impresa landing page

Create `/radar-impresa` as the explanatory public page. It does not duplicate the assessment and does not collect data.

Sections:

- What the Radar measures.
- The five departments.
- The four outputs: overall profile, process maturity, owner autonomy and AI adoption.
- Sector-aware interpretation.
- What happens after completion: result, Horyzon debrief and first priorities.
- What it is not: no diagnosis by itself, no automatic consulting plan, no guaranteed outcome.
- Direct action to `https://hub.horyzon.it/radar`.

### How Horyzon works

Reframe the existing method into a nine-stage journey:

1. Define destination and business goals.
2. Read the current state through Radar and discovery.
3. Design the target organization.
4. Map existing people and responsibilities.
5. Compare current and target states.
6. Select the priority department.
7. Build the operational programme and Blueprint.
8. Activate tools, roles and measurements with evidence.
9. Review results and continue with the next priority.

### The business system

Create a single overview that presents the five departments as an organism, not five generic service cards:

- Administration: cash, margins, budgets and continuity.
- Production: delivery standards, quality and customer satisfaction.
- Sales: repeatable sales method, recurring revenue and delegated closing.
- Marketing: positioning, demand and a stable flow of qualified opportunities.
- People: roles, selection, development and responsibility.

Each department page uses the same semantic pattern: warning signs, desired condition, questions to investigate, operating interventions and evidence of progress.

### Organizational wellbeing

Position this as Horyzon's core consulting discipline. Focus on organization chart, leadership, roles, processes, delegation, routines, standards and the owner's evolution from operating bottleneck to strategic guide.

### Digital wellbeing

Describe it as the layer that makes the model usable:

- tools selected for real processes;
- ChatGPT Work organized by roles and projects;
- approved integrations and explicit activation states;
- final reports and structured outputs returned to Platform;
- permissions, evidence and audit;
- AI that supports people without replacing accountability.

Avoid claiming that Platform connects directly to every customer system or stores all raw operational data.

### Financial wellbeing

Connect financial wellbeing to continuity and value protection: financial visibility, resilience, alignment between company, owner and family, and coordination with qualified professionals. Keep regulatory qualifications and risk disclosures already present.

### Platform page

Create `/piattaforma` to explain the customer workspace truthfully:

- company overview;
- role-aware views;
- KPI and time-series widgets;
- operational snapshots;
- reports;
- synchronization status and errors;
- Blueprint and activation evidence;
- verified progress instead of unproven automation.

Use interface-inspired editorial diagrams built in HTML/CSS rather than screenshots containing real or synthetic customer data.

### Frank page

Reposition Frank around the Radar and business transformation:

- interpreter of the business system;
- guide for the Radar debrief;
- facilitator of goals, target organization and priority department;
- bridge between entrepreneurial ambition and a practical operating programme.

Add two actions:

- Start the Radar — direct to the Hub Radar.
- Understand the method — internal link to `/radar-impresa` or `/metodo`.

Do not invent credentials, results or case studies.

### People pages

Move from biography-first to responsibility-first profiles. Each person page explains the role in the Horyzon journey, the decisions that person supports, the stage of the method they own and the most relevant next action.

### Contact page

Offer three clear entry points:

- Start the Radar.
- Request a debrief after completing it.
- Describe the department or bottleneck that needs attention.

The existing mail-client flow can remain until a real server-side contact service is explicitly authorized.

### Library and insights

Use existing publications as proof of the intellectual model. Organize future content around owner autonomy, the five departments, organizational design, governed AI and continuity. Do not present unpublished material as available.

## Copy system

### Voice

- Direct and comprehensible to an SME owner.
- Visionary at section openings, operational immediately afterward.
- Specific nouns: department, role, procedure, indicator, evidence, decision.
- Human without vague wellbeing language.
- Confident without guarantees or exaggerated market claims.

### Repeated vocabulary

Use consistently: horizon, current state, business organism, five departments, owner autonomy, target organization, priority, operational programme, Blueprint, activation, evidence, progress.

Avoid overusing: transformation, innovation, ecosystem, synergy, excellence, revolutionary, fully automated.

### Calls to action

Actions must name the outcome:

- Start the Radar.
- See what it measures.
- Request the debrief.
- Explore the five departments.
- Understand the activation path.
- Talk about your priority department.

## Visual direction

Retain the established navy, ivory and muted gold identity, Cormorant-led editorial typography and scroll-linked horizon video. The memorable element becomes the transition from horizon to a living business organism: five department lines converge into a single operating direction.

Do not turn the redesign into a SaaS card catalogue. Use:

- wide editorial sequences;
- structural lines that represent dependencies;
- one interactive five-department organism;
- restrained interface fragments for Platform;
- real Radar geometry recreated from the five-area model without displaying user data;
- asymmetric text alignment consistent with the homepage motion.

Mobile must preserve reading order, touch targets, reduced-motion support and road/horizon framing.

## Technical implementation

### New public components

- `RadarStory`: five-axis explanatory visualization and result legend.
- `BusinessOrganism`: accessible five-department model with links.
- `OperatingJourney`: nine-stage method narrative.
- `PlatformStory`: truthful diagram of Hub, ChatGPT Work, external systems and Platform.
- `ProductTruth`: reusable status language for available, configured and evolving capabilities.

### Content model

Move the principal Italian narrative out of the large catch-all route component into typed content modules. Keep legal and regulated content unchanged unless only surrounding navigation is adjusted. Do not silently rewrite translated routes; translation becomes a separate content pass after the Italian source is approved.

### External Radar link

Canonical target: `https://hub.horyzon.it/radar`.

Requirements:

- use a single exported constant;
- open in the same tab by default so browser history remains natural;
- describe the transition to Hub before the action;
- never pass personal data in query parameters from the public website;
- track no assessment data in the website;
- include the link in homepage, `/radar-impresa`, Frank's profile and contact choices.

## Verification

- Confirm every internal route referenced by the new copy is prerendered.

- No video added to internal pages.

## Delivery boundaries

This increment changes only `horyzon-website`. Hub and Platform are read-only sources of product truth. No database migration, production data change, Vercel configuration, Hub/Platform deployment or DNS change is included. Website push and production deployment remain separate release actions.
